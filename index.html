FAQ

Everything you want to know
before subscribing.

Do I need to sign a contract? Can I cancel?
+
No contract. Cancel any time with no questions asked. Your first month also comes with a 30-day full refund guarantee — if you're not satisfied, we return every rupee.
How do I know my coordinator is trustworthy?
+
Every PeakAge coordinator is police-verified and background-checked before hire. 48 hours before the first visit, they send a personal video introduction to your parents. They carry a PeakAge digital ID with a verifiable QR code at every visit. You receive a written emergency protocol document outlining exactly what they do in any scenario — no abstract promises.
Does my parent need to install an app or learn new technology?
+
No. Your parents communicate with their coordinator via WhatsApp — something they already know. No hospital visits, no new apps, no learning curve. The family dashboard is for you (on your phone), not for them.
What happens in an actual emergency at 2am?
+
Your coordinator is reachable 24×7. In an emergency, they are at your parents' door in under 30 minutes with first-aid capability. They coordinate hospitals, ambulances, and local contacts. You are notified instantly and kept updated throughout. You receive a written emergency action plan when you sign up so there are no surprises on either side.
I'm abroad (NRI). Can I sign up for my parents in India?
+
Yes — PeakAge was built with NRI families in mind. Subscribe from anywhere in the world. Your parents get the care on the ground. You get weekly updates to your WhatsApp. Payment is online and in INR.
What cities do you currently operate in?
+
We are live in Bangalore. Lucknow launches in August 2026 — register now and we'll notify you the moment slots open. We're also expanding to Nagpur, Indore, Bhopal, and Jaipur. Express interest and we'll prioritise your parents' city for our next launch.
What does ₹133/day actually cover?
+
For ₹133/day (₹4,000/month), you get a dedicated coordinator, fortnightly home health checks (BP, sugar, ECG), doctor visits up to twice a month, physiotherapy twice a month, concierge runs up to four times a month, 24×7 emergency response, and weekly family updates. There are no per-visit fees and no surprise charges — everything is included in the flat monthly rate.
